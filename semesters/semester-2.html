Semester II

Advancing pedagogical skills, creative projects, and active classroom teaching.

Setting the Stage

Semester II Orientation

The orientation for Semester II provided a clear roadmap for the advanced challenges ahead. We transitioned from foundational theories to practical application, focusing heavily on creative lesson planning and preparing for our upcoming school internships.

It was a crucial session that aligned our cohort's goals and set expectations for the intensive pedagogy and art-in-education projects we would be tackling.

BS

Content cum pedagogy of Biological Science

In Semester II, the focus shifted towards advanced teaching methodologies. I presented complex topics like the "Basic Unit of Life" and cell structures using a combination of detailed visual charts and live chalkboard demonstrations to keep students engaged.

H

Content cum pedagogy of Hindi

Language pedagogy expanded beyond the textbook. I collaborated with peers for dynamic group presentations and utilized creative environmental campaigns to integrate social awareness with language learning.

Projects like the "Ped Lagao, Dhara Bachao" (Plant Trees, Save the Earth) initiative and the "Importance of Trees" charts helped students connect Hindi vocabulary with real-world ecological concepts.

Hands-on Workshops

Before creating our final teaching aids, we participated in practical workshops. Guided by our instructors, we explored various mediums like glass painting, watercolors, and 3D modeling.

These hands-on sessions equipped us with the skills to blend our pedagogy subjects with creative expression, resulting in a diverse portfolio of educational art.
