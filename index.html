About Me

Becoming a Teacher

I am a B.Ed student teacher pursuing my professional preparation at R.G.R Siddanthi College of Education. My teacher education journey has helped me connect educational theory with practical classroom situations and understand the responsibilities that come with being a teacher.

Throughout my semesters, I have participated in seminars, pedagogical activities, creative teaching-aid development, peer teaching, school internship, and other academic experiences. Each of these experiences has contributed to my confidence, communication skills, classroom awareness, and professional growth.

This portfolio brings those experiences together as a record of my development as a student teacher and as a reflection of the teacher I am working towards becoming.
