Hands-on Creative Workshops

Art is not just a separate subject; it is an essential medium for effective teaching. Through hands-on training workshops, we explored various visual techniques—ranging from glass and bottle painting to tactile 3D models.

These sessions equipped us with the creative tools needed to make complex theoretical concepts interactive, visually striking, and memorable for school students.
