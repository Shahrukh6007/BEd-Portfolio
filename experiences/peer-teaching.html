Collaborative Growth

Peer Teaching & Micro-Teaching

Refining instructional delivery, testing lesson plans, and receiving constructive feedback within a collaborative student-teacher cohort.

🤝

The Power of Peer Feedback

Before stepping into live school classrooms, peer-teaching sessions provided a safe and dynamic environment to test out lesson plans, delivery pacing, and questioning techniques.

By teaching lessons to fellow student-teachers who acted as an active student audience, I learned how to adapt content on the fly, manage time effectively, and handle constructive evaluations.

Core Focus Areas

  • Micro-teaching skill integration (reinforcement, probing questions)
  • Chalkboard writing clarity and visual aid positioning
  • Peer critique and iterative lesson plan improvement
Professional Development

Learning Through Collaboration

Peer teaching helped transform theoretical knowledge into practical teaching behaviour. Each demonstration became an opportunity to observe different instructional approaches, identify strengths, and reflect on areas that could be improved.

Feedback from fellow student-teachers encouraged continuous refinement of communication, questioning, classroom presence, and the effective use of teaching-learning materials.

Time Management

Practising lesson delivery helped develop better pacing and the ability to complete planned activities within the available classroom time.

Communication

Repeated teaching demonstrations strengthened verbal communication, questioning techniques, explanation, and interaction with learners.

Reflection

Constructive peer evaluation encouraged reflective practice and helped identify specific areas for improvement in lesson delivery.

Personal Reflection

From Practice to Confidence

Peer teaching gave me the confidence to experiment with different teaching strategies before applying them in real classroom situations. It helped me understand that effective teaching involves preparation, flexibility, communication, and continuous reflection.

The experience also strengthened my ability to receive feedback positively and use it to improve my teaching practice. Working collaboratively with fellow student-teachers made the learning process more interactive and meaningful.
